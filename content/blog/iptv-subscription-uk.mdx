## Frequently asked questions: IPTV subscription UK

**Is an IPTV subscription legal in the UK?**
The technology is legal — it's the same streaming method broadcasters use in their own apps. Legality comes down to the provider's content licensing, so stick with a reputable service that can speak to how its content is licensed.

**How much does a UK IPTV subscription cost?**
A good **IPTV subscription UK** service starts at £15 a month with IPTV UK, or about £5 a month over a year — for 50,000+ channels and 200,000+ on-demand titles. A satellite package with fewer channels tends to run £65–£90 a month.

**Does IPTV work on my Smart TV?**
Yes. Samsung and LG sets use Smart IPTV or SSIPTV from their app stores; Android TVs (Sony, Philips and others) run TiviMate and IPTV Smarters directly.

**How fast does my internet need to be?**
At least 10 Mbps for HD and 25 Mbps for 4K. Most UK broadband clears that comfortably — even a standard 35 Mbps fibre line handles 4K fine.

**Can I try IPTV UK before subscribing?**
Yes — message us on WhatsApp for a free 3-hour trial. No card, no commitment; you'll have your login within minutes to test on your own setup.

**How many devices can I use at once?**
A standard subscription covers one connection at a time. Multi-connection plans (up to four) are available if you need to stream on more than one device simultaneously.
